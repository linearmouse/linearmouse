Firefox autoscroll acceleration curve
=====================================

LinearMouse's Auto Scroll speed follows the behavior of Firefox's autoscroll:
the pointer offset is measured in 12px steps, nothing scrolls within the first
step, and beyond it the distance per 20ms grows with the number of steps to the
power of 1.5, minus one. The distance is scaled by the real elapsed time (capped
at 100ms per frame), and sub-pixel remainders carry over to the next frame.

LinearMouse implements this curve independently in Swift. No Firefox source
code is included. The behavior was taken from:
https://github.com/mozilla-firefox/firefox/blob/57c647e76c75cb37fd6f274587b8d1e88b2a3a20/toolkit/actors/AutoScrollChild.sys.mjs
